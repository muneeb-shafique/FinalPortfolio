Origins

The Origin

I was 11 when I opened a piece of software and thought — how does this actually work?

That question never left me.

By 14, I had an answer. I built MF Result, a C# desktop application that automated student result generation, ranking, and printing. Not a school project. Not a tutorial follow-along. A real product, sold to my school, Learning Zone. That was my first line of revenue from code.

Most people my age were still figuring out what they wanted to do. I already knew.

The Evolution

That same obsession with building things that actually work brought me to Data Science and AI. Not because it was trending. Because it was the next frontier worth obsessing over.

Now I'm at UET Lahore studying Data Science, and the curiosity that started at 11 has compounded into something bigger — models that learn, systems that think, and tools that solve problems people didn't even know could be automated.

Where I Am Now

I'm not waiting to graduate to start building. I never did.

From a C# app sold at 14 to offline agentic AI running on local hardware with no internet and no API keys — the through line has always been the same.

I see a problem. I build the solution. I ship it.
